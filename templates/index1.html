About Rainy Brain

Rainy Brain is a web app powered by a Machine Learning model that predicts whether it will rain the next day. The model uses historical weather data from Maharashtra, India, to provide accurate rainfall forecasts. Maharashtra experiences a diverse climate, with heavy monsoon rains between June and September, which are vital for agriculture and water resources. Seasonal variations, including dry spells and humid periods, affect rainfall patterns across the region. Accurate prediction helps farmers plan better and supports water management efforts to mitigate drought and flooding risks.
